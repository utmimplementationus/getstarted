Decode the acronyms

Glossary

The cohort's documents are dense with abbreviations. Here's a quick translation.

UTM UAS Traffic Management
The systems and services that coordinate drone flights so they can operate safely, especially beyond the pilot's line of sight.
UAS / UA Unmanned Aircraft (System)
The drone itself (UA) and everything needed to operate it (UAS).
USS / USP UAS Service Supplier / Provider
The company providing UTM services, connecting operators and deconflicting their flight paths.
BVLOS Beyond Visual Line of Sight
Flying a drone farther than the operator can see it, the complex operations this cohort enables.
SCD Strategic Coodination / Conflict Detection
Detecting and resolving conflicts between flight plans before takeoff, plus monitoring conformance. The cohort's core service.
NRID Network Remote ID
Broadcasting a drone's identity and location over the network, the cohort's second active service.
SDD Service Description Document
The document that defines exactly what a service must do and how it's tested.
RTM Requirements Traceability Matrix
A spreadsheet mapping each requirement to how it's verified: your compliance checklist.
DSS Discovery & Synchronization Service
The shared backbone that lets providers find and sync with each other's operations.
SLA Service Level Agreement
The agreement between a service provider and an operator defining the service and responsibilities.
ASTM F3548-21
The international consensus standard for UTM interoperability that the whole system is built on.
NTAP Near Term Approvals Process
The FAA pathway through which these services are expected to be accepted.
DFR · COA · SGI Authorizations
Drone-as-First-Responder operations; Certificate of Waiver or Authorization; Special Governmental Interest. These credentials can substantiate eligibility for high-priority flights.
KML
A geographic file format used to capture the volumes of airspace a simulation covered.
